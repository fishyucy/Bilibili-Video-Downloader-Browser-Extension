为什么需要「访问所有网站」的权限？ / Why "access to all websites"?

· 读取 B 站视频页的音视频档位，并抓取你本来就能在网页上播放的流；
· B 站音视频 CDN 的域名是动态的，所以权限需要覆盖所有网站，而不是某一个域名；
· 除 B 站相关域名外，扩展不会访问其它网站；代码全开源，可随时审查。

· Reads the stream tiers of Bilibili video pages and fetches the streams you can already play there;
· Bilibili's media CDN hostnames are dynamic, so the permission must cover all sites rather than a single domain;
· Apart from Bilibili-related hosts the extension contacts nothing. The code is open source and auditable.

Firefox 把这项权限列为「可选权限」，安装后可能尚未开启 —— 点上面的按钮授权即可 （随时可在 about:addons 里关闭）。授权后请 刷新已打开的 B 站视频页（内容脚本需要重新注入），或 打开 bilibili.com 试试。

Firefox lists this as an optional permission; it may not be enabled right after installation -- click the button above to grant it (revocable anytime in about:addons). After granting, reload any open Bilibili video page (the content script must be injected again), or open bilibili.com to try.
