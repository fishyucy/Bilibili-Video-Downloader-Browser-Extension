关于本包可能内含的 ffmpeg.exe
About the ffmpeg.exe this archive may contain

  只有文件名带 -full 的压缩包（bili-dl-native-host-vX.Y.Z-full.zip）里才带 ffmpeg.exe。
  没有 -full 的精简包不含它，你可以：
    · 运行 find-ffmpeg.exe，让它在你的电脑里找一份现成的，或
    · 运行 get-ffmpeg.cmd，从网上下载一份，或
    · 直接把自己的 ffmpeg.exe 放进 native-host\bin\ 下

  Only the archive whose name ends in "-full" bundles ffmpeg.exe. The slim archive
  (without "-full") does not; in that case you can run find-ffmpeg.exe to locate one
  already on your machine, run get-ffmpeg.cmd to download one, or simply drop your own
  ffmpeg.exe into native-host\bin\.


来源 / Source

      https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
